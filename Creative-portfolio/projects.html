3D Diorama – full project

This diorama is a stylised representation of my own room: a personal space rebuilt in 3D to explore light, atmosphere and emotional storytelling through composition.

The project focuses on colour psychology, spatial rhythm and low-light ambience to evoke a calm, late-evening mood.

Exploración visual – full project

A small study on shape, colour and motion. Created as a personal exercise to experiment with balance, contrast and soft gradients.

Experimental piece – full view

A sequence of abstract compositions based on texture overlays, chromatic harmony and non-literal storytelling.
